Before you get started

UniTrack can help you understand activity during online assignments. Please review what it records before enabling tracking.

When enabled, it records

  • Active and idle time on websites you allow
  • Counts of typed and pasted characters
  • Paste event sizes and tab switches
  • Optional short paste previews, which you can turn off in Settings

It does not record passwords or send your activity to a server. Data is stored locally in this Chrome profile and can be deleted from Settings.
